How do I download a YouTube video?

To download a YouTube video, simply enter the video URL in the input field and click the download button.

What formats can I download videos in?

You can download videos in MP4 format and audio in MP3 format.

Is there a limit to how many videos I can download?

There is a rate limit of 100 requests per 15 minutes to prevent abuse.

Can I download videos from other platforms?

Currently, our service only supports downloading videos from YouTube.

What should I do if I encounter an error?

If you encounter an error, please check if the video is still available on YouTube. If the problem persists, contact our support team.

Can't find the answer you're looking for?

If you can't find the answer you're looking for, please contact our support team by visiting our support page.
